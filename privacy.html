Privacy Policy

Overview

FlightMode AI is designed around local, offline use. In this MVP, conversations are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to the backend.

Information We Collect

For account verification, we collect your email address, verification status, device identifier, platform, and Offline AI readiness status. We do not collect conversation content in this MVP.

Email Verification

Verification emails are sent by the backend service using the project email account. Verification codes expire after 10 minutes and are stored only as secure hashes.

Local Data

Your conversations and Offline AI package status are stored on your device. Deleting app data or uninstalling the app may remove local conversations.

Backend Data

The backend stores account and device readiness data in PostgreSQL. Backend credentials and email credentials are stored server-side only and are not included in the iOS app.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact skyhkgapp@gmail.com.
